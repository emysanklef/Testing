About the lodge

A hearth for every season

Ember & Oak began in 2011 as a single hunting cabin, rebuilt log by log into a twenty-two room lodge. We kept the things that mattered-the wood stoves, the long communal table, the sound of the creek from the porch - and added the things our guests asked for: proper water pressure, fast wifi in the lounge, and a kitchen that never really closes.

We're a family run team of eleven, most of whom grew up within a day's hike of the property. Booking with us means booking with people who actually know which trail catches the sunrise and which room has the quietest window.

  • Locally sourced breakfast, included every stay
  • Pet-friendly rooms on the ground floor
  • Free shuttle to the trailhead & village
