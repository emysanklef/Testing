Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Reach us anytime through the contact details in the footer.

What time is check-in and Check-out?

Check-in starts at 3:00 PM and Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early Check-in or late Check-out can be arranged for a small fee, subject to availability - just ask at booking.

Is breakfast really included in every rate?

Yes. A full breakfast is included with every room type, served in the Hearth Kitchen from 7:00 to 10:30 AM daily, with a packed option for early hikers.

Can i bring my dog?

Absolutely - our ground floor rooms are pet-friendly with no extra cleaning fee. Just mention it when booking so we can assign the right room.

Whats's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 5 days before check-in. Cancellations within 5 days are charged for the first night only, refunded to your original payment method.

Do you offer airport or village transfers?

We run a free shuttle to the village three times daily. Airport transfers can be arranged seperately for a flat fee - ask the front desk once your dates are confirmed.
